Verba - a voice agent that is not allowed to lie

Every answer is validated against a formal business ontology before it is spoken. One ontology, two channels: the phone line and the owner's chat. Sample business: Trattoria Luce (open Mon-Sat 11:00-22:00, with a confirmed private-event booking on Sunday Dec 13).
This page is a scripted browser replay of the Python prototype's real output (video | repo). Telephony, WhatsApp transport and the LLM are simulated; the ontology, validation and cross-channel logic are real code in the repo.
Eval on 6 probes with known ground truth: naive draft 0/6 correct, validated answer 6/6 correct (scripts/eval.py, enforced in CI).
